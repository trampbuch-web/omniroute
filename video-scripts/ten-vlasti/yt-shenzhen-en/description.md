Putin, Trump and Xi at One Table: The Full Story of the First-Ever Russia–China–US Summit | Shadow of Power

On November 19, 2026, in Shenzhen, the leaders of Russia, China and the United States sat at one table for the first time in history. Ninety minutes of talks, a "Declaration on Peace" — and not a single word about Ukraine.

In this episode of Shadow of Power: the full story. Why Europe feared a "new Yalta", how two opposite accounts of the same meeting emerged two weeks before the summit, what happened in Shenzhen, what the leaders signed, what Putin said at his press conference, how Washington, Berlin, Warsaw and NATO reacted — and what comes next.

We mark on screen what is confirmed and what is only a version. Assessments and forecasts are analysts' opinions, not facts.
